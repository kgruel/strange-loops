### Actionable Findings

#### Finding 1: [`prepare_declaration_edit`](file:///tmp/loops-arrival-correctness/review-stage1/libs/engine/src/engine/arrival_declarations.py#L810-L840) Omits Shared Anchor Validation, Accepting Foreign/Tainted Declaration Anchors
- **Severity**: High
- **Trigger**: An Arrival store's derived projection contains a foreign declaration anchor (`store_meta.own_lineage` set to a foreign lineage that has a corresponding `_decl.genesis` fact in `facts`, e.g., via merged fact events or projection corruption) while the underlying log custody remains at its legitimate physical lineage.
- **Actual Behavior**: [`open_read`](file:///tmp/loops-arrival-correctness/review-stage1/libs/engine/src/engine/arrival_consumer.py#L163) and [`capture_runtime`](file:///tmp/loops-arrival-correctness/review-stage1/libs/engine/src/engine/runtime_write.py#L736) reject this state with [`NotAuthority`](file:///tmp/loops-arrival-correctness/review-stage1/libs/engine/src/engine/arrival_contract.py#L98) via [`validate_arrival_declaration_anchor`](file:///tmp/loops-arrival-correctness/review-stage1/libs/engine/src/engine/declaration.py#L197-L211). However, [`prepare_declaration_edit`](file:///tmp/loops-arrival-correctness/review-stage1/libs/engine/src/engine/arrival_declarations.py#L814-L835) opens `query.open_snapshot` and directly passes `snapshot.declaration_anchor` to [`resolve_declaration_documents_from_snapshot`](file:///tmp/loops-arrival-correctness/review-stage1/libs/engine/src/engine/declaration.py#L213-L260) without invoking [`validate_arrival_declaration_anchor`](file:///tmp/loops-arrival-correctness/review-stage1/libs/engine/src/engine/declaration.py#L197-L211). It resolves documents belonging to the foreign lineage, diffs them against proposed edits, and prepares a signed plan against the store.
- **Expected Behavior**: [`prepare_declaration_edit`](file:///tmp/loops-arrival-correctness/review-stage1/libs/engine/src/engine/arrival_declarations.py#L827-L835) must call `validate_arrival_declaration_anchor(snapshot.declaration_anchor, captured_head)` immediately after opening the snapshot to refuse foreign declaration state before document diffing.
- **Source Location**: [arrival_declarations.py:L814-835](file:///tmp/loops-arrival-correctness/review-stage1/libs/engine/src/engine/arrival_declarations.py#L814-L835)
- **Proof**: Executed disposable store probe `/tmp/loops-arrival-correctness/stage1-probes/probe_decl_edit_foreign.py`. With `store_meta.own_lineage` set to a foreign lineage with matching foreign genesis, `prepare_declaration_edit` succeeded:
  ```

  Plan captured_head lineage: 01M1TD0A0SAFT0FJ8D5ZH0WVD9
  Plan basis lineage: 01M1TD0A0SAFT0FJ8D5ZH0WVD9
  Plan changes: ()
  ```

#### Finding 2: [`validate_arrival_declaration_anchor`](file:///tmp/loops-arrival-correctness/review-stage1/libs/engine/src/engine/declaration.py#L197-L211) Does Not Enforce Genesis Kind When Called Standalone
- **Severity**: Low
- **Trigger**: Directly invoking [`plan_ordinary_write`](file:///tmp/loops-arrival-correctness/review-stage1/libs/engine/src/engine/runtime_write.py#L834-L840) or consumer entry points with an unadopted or synthetic snapshot whose `anchor.genesis` is an ordinary fact (`kind != "_decl.genesis"`) that shares the lineage ID.
- **Actual Behavior**: [`validate_arrival_declaration_anchor`](file:///tmp/loops-arrival-correctness/review-stage1/libs/engine/src/engine/declaration.py#L197-L211) validates only `anchor.genesis.id == head.lineage` and `anchor.own_lineage == head.lineage`, omitting a check for `anchor.genesis.kind == DECL_GENESIS`. While full candidate hydration in [`_build_effective_arrival_candidate`](file:///tmp/loops-arrival-correctness/review-stage1/libs/engine/src/engine/runtime_write.py#L737) invokes [`resolve_declaration_documents_from_snapshot`](file:///tmp/loops-arrival-correctness/review-stage1/libs/engine/src/engine/declaration.py#L236) (which checks `DECL_GENESIS`), callers relying exclusively on [`validate_arrival_declaration_anchor`](file:///tmp/loops-arrival-correctness/review-stage1/libs/engine/src/engine/declaration.py#L197-L211) accept non-genesis facts as identity genesis anchors.
- **Expected Behavior**: [`validate_arrival_declaration_anchor`](file:///tmp/loops-arrival-correctness/review-stage1/libs/engine/src/engine/declaration.py#L207-L211) should verify `anchor.genesis.kind == DECL_GENESIS` whenever `anchor.genesis is not None`.
- **Source Location**: [declaration.py:L207-211](file:///tmp/loops-arrival-correctness/review-stage1/libs/engine/src/engine/declaration.py#L207-L211)
- **Proof**: Calling `validate_arrival_declaration_anchor(DeclarationAnchor(own_lineage=h.lineage, genesis=Fact(id=h.lineage, kind="note", ...)), h)` passes without raising `NotAuthority`.
